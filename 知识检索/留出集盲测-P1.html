## 8. Thinking: bind-mount vs overlay investigation (step 231)
- **Common**: both use /proc/mountinfo as the source of truth, decompose a mount into source/root/options → verify backing exists, cross-check with multiple toolsm (findmnt/lsblk/stat/python), and record errors honestly; both address to Panshi/OSTree design.
- **Difference**: overlay = **vertical stacking** on ONE target (which layer is effective; identity by inode/stat; per-layer ro/rw/depth). Bind = **horizontal multi-mounts** of one device; the key is mountinfo **field-4 (root subtree)** to re-map a device's mounts back to `/data/home→/home`, `/data/root→/root`.
- Same under hood (read mountinfo + resolve), different geometry (stacking vs aliasing) — that's the crux.
